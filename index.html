Faith & Trust

  • Christian, God did not save you to start the race. He saved you so that you might finish the race.
  • It’s not about more faith, it’s about the right kind of faith. God give me the right kind of faith to praise you, worship you, forgive others, and love the way you love.
  • Jesus calls us to a living, relational trust in God rather than a mere intellectual belief. This right kind of faith is characterized by active obedience, complete dependence on His grace, and courage to step out in love.
  • Stop thinking about how I can do it. Start thinking about how He has already done it. Rely on God and the impossible will be possible.
  • The way we pursue righteousness in love is to FIGHT for faith in future grace.
  • The key to patience is holding out by the promises of Jesus. This is holding onto faith in future grace.
  • Faith is the experience of contentment in Jesus. The fight of faith is the fight to keep your heart contented with Jesus.
  • "I believe in Christianity as I believe that the Sun has risen: not only because I see it, but because by it I see everything else." — C.S. Lewis
  • "Count it all joy, my brothers, when you meet trials of various kinds, for you know that the testing of your faith produces steadfastness."
  • Surrendering to the Lord isn’t losing control of your life, but trusting the one who sees the full picture.
  • A stronghold is a false premise that denies God’s promise. Miracles don’t produce faith. Faith produces miracles.

God & His Character

  • Even though my steps forward aren’t the best, they are still steps forward. Weakness is not the end of us; it’s the beginning of God’s might within us.
  • God does not have favorites—He has specifics.
  • God is a God of "and suddenly".
  • God doesn’t want you clean right now; He wants you as you are. God does the cleaning—He wipes your sins away.
  • God’s approval doesn’t change. He always loves me and everyone else. Nothing compares to the scope of God’s love.
  • In the moment you don’t feel God at all could be the closest He’s ever been to you. — Billy Graham
  • Disappointment requires surprise. God is not surprised; He knows everything you’re going to do and He still loves you.
  • Lower your expectation of how He will speak to you, because He is already speaking to you. Sometimes I expect fireworks, but my wonderful God is talking in quiet whispers.

Jesus Christ

  • Peace begins the moment you get off the throne and let Jesus sit on it and let Him be King.
  • Wherever you go, you are an ambassador for Christ.
  • My identity is not in what I have done, but it is in who Jesus says I am!
  • Jesus died for the worst version of myself that I hate the most.
  • We are dirt. People walk on dirt and spit on it, but with the right gardener dirt can produce beautiful things. Thank you Jesus for being my gardener.
  • Faith doesn’t make anxiety disappear; faith gives me somewhere to take it. That is my Lord Jesus.
  • Being in Christ means we are no longer slaves to a life weighed down by complicated rules. We are free from the weight of perfection.

Prayer, Worship & Relationship With God

  • Prayer is essential for the life of a believer. God is worthy of prayer and worship.
  • "Draw near to God, and he will draw near to you."
  • That one-on-one connection with Jesus is what matters heavily when wanting to get closer to God. Jesus would often talk to God alone for long periods.
  • Talk to God every day—He wants to hear you.

Sin, Repentance & Forgiveness

  • Your pride is the devil’s weapon, not yours.
  • When conviction hits you, let it grieve and correct you. But don’t chew on guilt and shame, because shame is already taken away by Jesus.
  • Repentance is not dictated by what you feel while sinning, but how you feel after you committed the sin.
  • The most crucial part of repentance isn't asking for forgiveness (because that's already given), but your resolution to avoid sinning in the future. I repent so my heart can be aligned with Your heart!

Holiness, Obedience & Discipleship

  • It is our duty to serve Jesus. We don’t serve Him because we’re doing something for Him—it’s because we are obliged to follow in His footsteps.
  • We live by faith alone, but faith is not alone; it is accompanied by good works.
  • It’s important that we remind ourselves that we are saved FOR good works, not BY good works.

Wisdom & Scripture

  • Memorizing Scripture is a good defense against temptation. A Bible on a shelf is like a sword unsheathed.
  • Stop living based off feelings and start living off the truth that is written. Be a doer who acts on His words.
  • Today, let’s get in the Word—before the world gets into us!
  • "How can a young man keep his way pure? By guarding it according to your word."

Mind, Emotions & Identity

  • I want to do things out of the tenacity that God has provided in me, not fear. Fear is a weapon of the enemy.
  • I am loved and uniquely valued. Not worthless.
  • Worry does not empty tomorrow of its sorrow; it empties today of its strength. — Corrie ten Boom

Love, Mercy & Relationships

  • God doesn’t need things from me. He wants my heart, my love, and to be in a relationship with me.
  • Kindness is valuing people so highly that I refuse to engage in fault-finding.
  • "A friend loves at all times, and a brother is born for adversity." "A man of many companions may come to ruin, but there is a friend who sticks closer than a brother."
  • Mercy triumphs over judgment. True power is in forgiveness.

Suffering, Trials & Perseverance

  • Affliction produces steadfast character within us. When we choose to endure through suffering, Jesus forms strength within us.
  • There’s no need to fight battles that haven’t happened yet. God said worry about today because it has its own problems.
  • Fight impatience with Scripture, just like any other temptation.

Money, Contentment & Worldly Desires

  • Covetousness is desiring something so much that you lose your contentment in God. The opposite of covetousness is contentment with God.
  • Covetousness is a heart divided between two gods; so Paul calls it idolatry.
  • Be content no matter what. Let compassion and contentment rule over judgment.

Purpose, Calling & Christian Life

  • It’s not about if you’re chosen—it’s about when you answer the call. We were made on purpose, so let’s live like it.
  • "For you were called to freedom, brothers. Only do not use your freedom as an opportunity for the flesh, but through love serve one another."

Holy Spirit

  • When the Holy Spirit controls your heart, it leads to life and peace.
  • "If we live by the Spirit, let us also keep in step with the Spirit." Lord Jesus, give me strength to keep in step with the Holy Spirit.

Bible Studies & Verses

  • Key Passages: Luke 2:30-32, James 5:12, Hebrews 13:5-6, Philippians 4:11-13
  • Proverbs 21:1 — "The King’s heart is a stream of water in the hand of the Lord; He turns it wherever He wants."
  • Psalm 73:26 — "God is the strength of my heart and my portion forever!"
  • God knows each of us down to the last hair on our head (Matthew 10:30).
